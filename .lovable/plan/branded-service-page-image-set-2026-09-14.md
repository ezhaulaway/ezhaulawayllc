# Branded Service-Page Image Set

## Direction
Create a cohesive set of 12 photorealistic job images for EZ Haulaway. Every image will feel like the same local Colorado company and use:

- A red heavy-duty pickup and black tandem-axle dump trailer
- Red, black, and charcoal workwear on a generic professional crew
- Realistic Parker/Denver-area homes, yards, garages, and businesses
- Natural daylight and documentary-style photography rather than stock-photo posing
- A consistent wide 4:3 composition that works in every service-page header

The real EZ Haulaway logo will remain a clean, exact website overlay rather than asking image generation to redraw the lettering inaccurately.

## Images
Generate one distinct scene for each page:

1. Furniture Removal — crew carrying a sectional from a home toward the trailer
2. Appliance Removal — refrigerator on an appliance dolly beside the trailer
3. Hot Tub Removal — dismantled spa sections being carried from a backyard
4. Light Demolition — crew removing a small shed or playset with tools and debris
5. Deck & Fence Removal — dismantled deck boards and railing being loaded
6. Construction Debris Removal — organized lumber, drywall, and renovation debris at a jobsite
7. Yard & Landscaping Debris — branches, brush, and yard waste being loaded
8. Garage Cleanouts — open garage changing from cluttered to cleared, with sorted items ready to load
9. House & Estate Cleanouts — respectful whole-home cleanout with furniture and labeled donation items
10. Mattress & Bulky Item Removal — crew safely carrying a wrapped mattress and bulky item
11. Electronics & E-Waste Removal — TVs, monitors, and computers organized for responsible recycling
12. Commercial Junk Removal — office furniture and fixtures being removed from a small business

## Website Update
- Save the new images as project assets and assign the correct image to every service page.
- Add a restrained EZ Haulaway logo badge over each service image using the real logo asset, preserving exact branding.
- Keep the current page layout, text, navigation, and quote forms unchanged.
- Update image descriptions for accessibility.
- Verify the service pages on desktop and mobile, checking crops, branding, and readability.

## Technical Details
- Generate at a consistent 4:3 landscape ratio with enough safe space for responsive cropping.
- Use lazy loading and explicit dimensions below the first screen where appropriate.
- Avoid visible generated words, license plates, unsafe work practices, branded third-party products, and recognizable individuals.
