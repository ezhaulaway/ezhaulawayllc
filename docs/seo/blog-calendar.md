## Image style (short)

Real phone photo from a job. Landscape 16:9, daylight, item in context, EZ trailer or F-350 in frame when it fits. No stock, no AI, no text overlays, no faces, no house numbers, no plates, no street signs. Drop the photo in chat by Monday night; it is saved under `/workspace/blog-images/<slug>/`.

## Interview questions already written

Answers go word for word in `/workspace/blog-interviews/<slug>.md`.

**Dec 1** (`what-can-be-donated`):

1. Think of a couch or dresser you hauled that was still in good shape, and one that had to go to the dump. What made the difference?
2. When a customer asks "where's my furniture going to end up?", what do you tell them? Say it the way you'd say it to them.
3. Which donation spots in Denver or the south metro have you actually dropped furniture at, and what do they take or turn away?
4. What do people get wrong about donating furniture, like condition, who'll take a sofa, or thinking it's cheaper to donate than to have it hauled?

**Dec 8** (`holiday-cleanouts-parker`):

1. Tell me about a holiday-week or pre-Christmas cleanout you remember. What were people trying to get out of the house, and how tight was the timing?
2. When someone texts in mid-December and says they need it gone before family arrives, what do you tell them about timing and price? Say it the way you'd say it to them.
3. What piles up around the holidays that people don't think of until guests are coming, like boxes, old decorations, or garage overflow?
4. What should people have ready so a holiday-week pickup goes fast?
