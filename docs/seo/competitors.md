A real idea becomes a new entry in [CHANGE-REQUESTS.md](CHANGE-REQUESTS.md) with `Proposed by: Competitor Research`, evidence from a row above, and status `proposed`. 1. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Add Parker sub-pages for furniture removal, hot tub removal, appliance removal, mattress and bulky items, house and estate cleanouts, and shed removal. Evidence: Parker has two sub-pages today, Castle Rock and Centennial have five or more (https://www.ezhaulawayllc.com/sitemap.xml, 2026-11-26), and Rockies Cleanouts shows up for those searches with exactly these pages.
2. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Rewrite the repeated boilerplate headings on Parker, Highlands Ranch, Lone Tree and Elizabeth so each town page has its own neighborhoods, real job photos, nearest disposal site and a review from that town. The EZ Crew site check (2026-11-19) flags the city pages as near copies.
3. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Put a starting price and "same-day" in town page titles (for example "from $120"), since LoadUp, Lone Tree Junk and 1-800-GOT-JUNK do. Keep every price equal to the live price list.
4. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Build a dedicated same-day page only if Ben can honor a same-day cutoff time, and state the cutoff.
5. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Add a Parker cost guide and add structured data (Service, Offer, FAQ) to /pricing and /what-we-take, which have none (checked 2026-11-26).
6. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Add short "local rules" guides (for example bulk trash options in Parker and Castle Pines, what Douglas County disposal sites take). Only publish facts checked against the city or county source.
7. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Pick one page to own "junk removal parker co" (home or /service-areas/parker) so the two do not compete, and link to it with that wording.
8. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Reply to the 7 Google reviews that have no owner reply (EZ Crew read 2026-11-26) and keep asking each customer for a review that names the job and the town. EZ has 22 reviews at 5.0 (EZ Crew read 2026-11-26), against 123 for Parker Junk Removal.
9. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Check whether Sunday hours or "Sunday by appointment" fit the schedule. The site markup shows Monday to Saturday 7am to 7pm, while Parker Junk Removal is open Tuesday to Sunday.
10. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Make the dirt, rock, mulch and sod pages more prominent. The only junk company seen publishing dirt and sod rates was Junkman (bed loads), so the junk-removal field is thin there. Demand for these terms was not measured.
11. Proposed by: Competitor Research: Decide whether Monument and Kiowa deserve effort. Both are far from Parker with separate local competitors, and EZ has no Search Console visibility there yet.
