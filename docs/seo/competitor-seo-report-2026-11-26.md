### What the stronger pages share

- Town name and a first-job promise in the title ("Same-Day", "From $75", "From $95")
- A price or price method on the page, often with FAQ markup
- A section on local rules or where things go (LoadUp, JDog, Lone Tree Junk)
- Neighborhood names, recent work and reviews from that town (Mountain Goat)
- A page per service per town (Rockies Cleanouts, Junk Rangers)
- Cost guides with a price table (Junk Rangers, Altitude, Junkman)

### Where the Parker-area rivals are weak

- Parker Junk Removal: 13 pages, no blog, no cost page, no FAQ markup
- Jolly Junk: Parker page is about 700 words, no schema, staging address in the home canonical tag
- Mountain Men: Parker page is about 500 words
- Rockies Cleanouts: no schema and no H1 on service pages
- Out of Space: four pages, no town pages
- Almost none publish a hot tub, shed or dirt price

## Action list

Recommendations only. The EZ SEO & Blog teammate handles the website code, and nothing has been changed.

### This week (free, no code)

1. **Reply to your 7 unanswered Google reviews.** Name the job and the town, like "thanks for trusting us with the hot tub in Parker". Replies are free and Google lists them as a tip.
2. **Text your review link after every job.** Ask them to mention what you hauled and the town. Target 50 reviews by spring. Spread them out; steady beats a burst.
3. **Check your Google profile by hand.** Primary category "Junk Removal Service", the right extra categories, hours (add Sunday or "Sunday by appointment" if you really take them), 10+ real job photos, services with "from" prices, a service area centered on Parker. Remove the tracking tags from the website link.
4. **Do not list towns you will not drive to.** Monument and Kiowa are far from Parker and have their own local rivals. Keep them if you want those jobs, but do not build pages for them first.

### Next 2 to 4 weeks (website)

1. **Add Parker pages that match your Denver ones.** Furniture removal, hot tub removal, appliance removal, mattress and bulky items, house and estate cleanouts, shed removal. Each with a photo of a real Parker job, a price and four or five real questions.
2. **Rewrite the repeated town-page sections.** Give Parker, Highlands Ranch, Lone Tree and Elizabeth their own neighborhoods, a local job, the nearest disposal site, and a review from that town.
3. **Put a starting-price token in town titles, and a same-day note only where you can honor it.** See the plan below. Keep every price the same as your live price list, using price tokens on the live site.
4. **Add structured data to /pricing and /what-we-take.** Service, offer and FAQ markup, like your town pages already have.
5. **Build one same-day page, only if you can state a cutoff.** For example "call by noon". If you cannot promise it, skip this.
6. **Pick one page to own "junk removal parker co".** Your home page and your Parker page compete for it. Link from the home page to the Parker page with that wording and watch for 4 weeks.

### Next 1 to 3 months

1. **Write local-rules guides, after checking the facts.** Bulk trash options in Parker and Castle Pines, what Douglas County sites take. Rivals use these to look local. Verify every rule with the town or county page before publishing.
2. **Earn local links.** You already list the Parker Chamber, BBB and Nextdoor. Add realtors, property managers, estate sale companies, donation partners and the Castle Rock and Highlands Ranch chambers.
3. **Push Castle Rock.** It has the most rivals and your lowest position. Ask Castle Rock customers for reviews that say Castle Rock, and link to the Castle Rock page from related blog posts.
4. **Promote dirt, rock, mulch and sod removal.** Almost no junk company publishes rates for it. Demand was not measured, so test one page and watch Search Console.
